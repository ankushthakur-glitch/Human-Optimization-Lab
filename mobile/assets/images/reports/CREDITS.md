# Report images

Real, de-identified scans from Wikimedia Commons, used as sample images for the patient app's imaging reports. None is the sample patient's own scan. The `*-report.jpg` pages are drawn placeholders.
Licences that require it (CC BY, CC BY-SA) need this credit kept with the images. Resized; `echo-1.jpg` is cropped to remove a caption.
