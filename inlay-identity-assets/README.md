# Inlay Health — identity assets (v2.1, September 2026)

Companion files to the brand identity deck.

## logo/
The Assembled Mark and its lockups as outlined vector artwork (no live text) and PNG.

- `svg/` — master artwork. Symbol, primary lockup, horizontal lockup and app icon,
  each in full color, reversed (on petrol) and single color, on porcelain and on petrol.
  Symbol-only files carry the clear space inside the file.
- `png/` — 1024–2400 px exports. `-transparent` files have no background and are
  the ones placed in the deck; `-on-porcelain` / `-on-petrol` files carry the background.

Colors: petrol #173E45 · mist #DDE8E5 · stone #D8D2C8 · jade #8DADA5 · porcelain #F7F4EE.

Rules: supplied outlined artwork only. No retyping, gradients, rotation, stretching or
shadows. Never recolor pieces outside the palette, never separate or rearrange them,
never use the mark as a container for imagery.

Clear space: X = the width of the stone piece (about one third of the symbol's height).
Minimum sizes: symbol 32 px / 8 mm full color, 24 px / 6 mm single color; primary
lockup 200 px / 50 mm; horizontal lockup 160 px / 40 mm; app icon and favicon 24 px.

## tokens/
`inlay-tokens.css` and `inlay-tokens.json` — core palette, status colors, spacing,
radii, type scale and logo rules.

## contrast/
`inlay-contrast.csv` — WCAG relative-luminance contrast ratios for the palette pairs
used in the deck, with AA text and non-text thresholds.

Name, symbol, domain and handles have not been cleared for launch. No trademark search
or production accessibility audit has been performed.
